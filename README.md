## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a product manager at **10 Minute School** in Dhaka, working at the intersection of product, content, engineering, and operations. My job is turning messy operational problems into PRDs, systems, and shipped features — the bottleneck in how videos get published, the content workflow that doesn't scale, the process held together by spreadsheets.

Here's the scale I work at day to day:

- 🎬 **30,000+** educational videos in production and migration
- 🎓 **~5,000+** LIVE classes run and supported
- 📝 **400K+** questions in the question bank
- 🧑‍🎓 **~5,000+** daily active users, kept live with near-zero downtime
- 🏗️ **5** internal platforms owned end-to-end

<br/>

## 🚀 What I Do

- 🧩 **Product Ownership** — full lifecycle for internal CMS & Question Bank platforms: PRD authoring, backlog prioritization, and cross-functional delivery with Design, Backend, Frontend, and QA.
- 🤖 **AI-Powered Systems** — built **TenTen**, a RAG-based knowledge base connecting 3,000+ content assets across five platforms, leading a team of 4 to produce 200+ AI-generated educational media assets.
- ☁️ **Platform Migration** — designed a Python automation pipeline that migrated 30,000+ videos from YouTube to AWS S3, achieving full platform independence with zero service disruption.
- 📚 **Learning Experience Products** — Free Class Booking, Homework Submission, and CQ/Written Exam Submission systems built for the real day-to-day of learning.
