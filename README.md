## About Me

I'm a product manager at **10 Minute School** in Dhaka, working at the point where product, content, engineering, and operations meet. My job is to take messy operational problems — a bottleneck in how videos get published, a content workflow that doesn't scale, a support process that's held together by spreadsheets — and turn them into PRDs, systems, and shipped features.

Over the past two years, that's meant owning the operations behind a genuinely large amount of infrastructure: **30,000+ educational videos**, **~5,000+ live classes**, a **question bank of 400K+ items**, and **~5,000+ daily active users**, kept running with almost zero downtime across five internal platforms.

<br/>

## What I Do

**Product ownership.** I run the full lifecycle for internal CMS and Question Bank platforms — writing PRDs, prioritizing the backlog, and coordinating delivery across Design, Backend, Frontend, and QA.

**AI-powered systems.** I built **TenTen**, a RAG-based knowledge base that connects 3,000+ content assets across five platforms, and led a team of four to produce 200+ AI-generated educational media assets on top of it.

**Platform migration.** I designed a Python automation pipeline that moved 30,000+ videos from YouTube to AWS S3 — full platform independence, zero service disruption.

**Learning experience products.** Free Class Booking, Homework Submission, and CQ/Written Exam Submission — systems built to make the actual day-to-day of learning smoother.
